Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Twitch Clipper ("the app") runs entirely on the user's own computer. The author operates no servers for the app and does not collect, store or sell personal data.

Data the app uses

  • TikTok authorization tokens. When the user connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and a refresh token. They are stored only in a file on the user's computer and are used solely to upload the user's own videos to that account.
  • Basic profile information. The app may read the account's display name to show which account is connected. It is not stored or shared.
  • Videos. Video files, transcripts and clips are processed and stored locally on the user's computer. A clip is sent only to TikTok, and only when the user chooses to post it.

Sharing

The app does not share data with third parties, and it has no analytics or advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the user's computer until the user deletes the token file or revokes the app's access in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and websites). After revocation the app can no longer access the account.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests regarding your data: please open an issue at github.com/Ascento33/twitch-clipper-site/issues.
