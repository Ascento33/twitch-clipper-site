Twitch Clipper

Twitch Clipper is a personal desktop tool. It cuts the author's own Twitch stream recordings into short vertical clips (20–30 seconds) with subtitles, and, after the account owner authorizes it through TikTok Login Kit, uploads those clips to the owner's own TikTok account.

How it works

  • The video is downloaded and processed locally on the owner's computer (speech recognition and cutting are done locally).
  • The owner reviews the clips in the app and chooses what to post.
  • Clips are uploaded only to the TikTok account that the owner has explicitly connected.

The app is for personal use and is not distributed to other users.
